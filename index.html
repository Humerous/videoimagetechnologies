01 / 08 — POSITIONING

DATA AND SYSTEMS NEED TO BE UNDERSTOOD BEFORE THEY CAN BE RELIED ON

Important data can become spread across drives, folders, storage systems, backups, cloud locations and archives.

Without clear indexing, verification and documentation, it can become difficult to know:

  • What data exists.
  • Where it is stored.
  • Whether transfers completed correctly.
  • Whether expected files are missing.
  • Whether backups are reliable.
  • Whether storage and archive systems remain appropriate.
  • Where technical or operational risks exist.

VIT provides structured technical services to assess the current position, organise and verify information, identify risks and establish an appropriate way forward.

Understand the data. Verify the position. Identify the risk. Improve the system. Document the result.
